Welcome to The CSE Cheese Society

Main

We are a community of students, staff, and cheese enthusiasts who gather to taste, learn about, and discuss cheeses from around the world. Whether you are an experienced cheese connoisseur or simply cheese-curious, you are welcome here.

Explore our cheese guide, learn more about the Society, or join us at an upcoming tasting.

Cheese is one of the world’s oldest prepared foods, with evidence of cheesemaking dating back thousands of years.

Cheese of the Month — This month, we are celebrating blue cheese—bold, distinctive, and full of character.
